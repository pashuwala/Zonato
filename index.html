Offgrid Solar System

Complete Energy Independence

Fully independent solar systems designed for locations with no grid connectivity or frequent power outages. Powered by next-generation lithium batteries, our offgrid systems deliver 24/7 uninterrupted energy — rain or shine.

  • Advanced LiFePO4 Lithium Battery Bank
  • High-efficiency MPPT Solar Charge Controller
  • Pure Sine Wave Inverter Technology
  • Battery Management System (BMS)
  • Remote Monitoring via Smart App
  • 10-Year System Performance Warranty
  • Ideal for Homes, Farms & Remote Industries
  • offgrid without backup

Hybrid Solar System

Grid + Solar + Battery

The smartest solar investment — a hybrid system seamlessly integrates solar panels, advanced lithium batteries and grid power. Automatically switches between sources for zero downtime, bill savings and backup during outages.

  • Dual Mode: Grid-Tied + Battery Backup
  • Advanced NMC/LiFePO4 Lithium Storage
  • Hybrid Inverter with Smart Load Management
  • Net Metering Ready — Sell excess power
  • AI-Optimised Energy Management System
  • Real-time Energy Dashboard & Analytics
  • For Homes, Offices & Commercial Setups
